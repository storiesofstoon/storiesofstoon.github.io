A restless troublemaker with a backwards cap, a BMX bike, and a dangerous fascination with fire. After learning his parents died in the disaster, Bug struggles to channel his grief into something other than destruction. He can improvise under pressure and rarely backs away from a fight, but his impulsiveness threatens the people closest to him.

Strengths: Survival instinct, improvisation, fearlessness.

Weaknesses: Recklessness, anger, fascination with fire.

Loud, energetic, and almost impossible to keep focused, Goose uses humour to cope with the city falling apart. His familiar jersey and unpredictable reactions make him easy to spot in a crowd. He is fiercely loyal to his friends, even when he has no idea what the plan is.

Strengths: Loyalty, quick reactions, morale.

Weaknesses: Panic, distractions, poor impulse control.

Quiet and level-headed, Blue notices what others miss. Wearing her familiar beanie and boots, she moves through the outbreak with a wary eye and a reluctance to trust strangers. When Bug and Goose charge forward, Blue is often the one asking what could go wrong—and figuring out how to get them back out.

Strengths: Observation, caution, clear thinking.

Weaknesses: Guardedness, difficulty trusting others.

The owner of Olde Books knows far more about Stoon’s history than a bookstore proprietor should. Behind his gruff, smoke-filled manner is a determined protector who becomes an unlikely guide to Bug, Goose, and Blue. His books, notes, and connections may help explain the symbols and the horrors beneath the cemetery.

Strengths: Knowledge, strategy, resourcefulness.

Weaknesses: Secrecy, stubbornness, old habits.

Marcus has decided that order will not return to Stoon on its own—someone has to take control. As leader of the Crowned, he claims territory, manages dangerous loyalties, and tries to turn a frightened group into an organization that can survive. His ambition brings security to some and danger to others.

Strengths: Leadership, nerve, tactical thinking.

Weaknesses: Pride, territorial ambition, fragile alliances.

Ricky disappears during the Crowned’s expansion and returns changed. When his former crew finds him among the undead, he behaves unlike the other infected. A strange stitched symbol on his jacket points toward forces operating beyond the Crowned’s understanding.

Strengths: An unsettling connection to the outbreak’s mystery.

Weaknesses: His condition and intentions are uncertain.

A hooded, wheelchair-bound elder at the centre of the group operating beneath the cemetery. Jeb speaks with the confidence of a man who believes he understands the city’s transformation. He prefers influence and calculated bargains to open conflict, but his plans for the dead remain deeply troubling.

Strengths: Influence, patience, long-term planning.

Weaknesses: Dependence on his inner circle, dangerous certainty.

Cyrus supervises the underground laboratory and its unsettling work with the infected. Clinical, focused, and willing to push boundaries, he views the outbreak as something to study—and possibly direct. His research links the facility, the strange symbols, and the zombies who do not behave as expected.

Strengths: Medical expertise, analytical thinking.

Weaknesses: Obsession, moral detachment.

Hunched and usually hidden beneath dark robes, Igor carries messages, escorts visitors, and guards the cult’s secrets. He knows the maze of stairways, corridors, and reinforced doors beneath the cemetery. Often underestimated, he is a constant presence wherever the cult needs eyes and muscle.
